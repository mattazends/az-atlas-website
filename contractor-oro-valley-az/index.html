Building and remodeling in Oro Valley

Oro Valley sits north of Tucson along Oracle Road, under Pusch Ridge and the Santa Catalinas. Most homes here are in planned communities like Rancho Vistoso, Sun City Oro Valley and Stone Canyon, which means two approvals on most projects: your HOA's architectural review, then the town's building permit. We plan for both from the first visit, so the paperwork doesn't stall the job.

Who issues permits in Oro Valley

Oro Valley is its own town, not part of the City of Tucson, so your permit comes from the Town of Oro Valley. Arizona's 2024 ADU law covers cities over 75,000 people, so casitas in Oro Valley follow the town's own code plus your HOA's rules.

What Oro Valley homeowners call us for

  • Custom homes and additions and casitas
  • Stucco repair and roof repair and replacement
  • Structural repair and framing
  • Remodels, bathrooms and custom showers and custom cabinets
  • Decks, covered patios and repairs

Common requests here include casitas and primary suites for visiting family, updating builder-grade bathrooms into walk-in showers, and stucco and tile roof repairs on homes that are 20 or 30 years into the desert sun.

Why one contractor matters in a planned community

HOAs care about matching: stucco texture, paint color and roof tile. Because our own crew does the framing, stucco and roofing, the finished work matches what the HOA approved. We're licensed with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (AZ ROC #344537), which HOAs and the town will ask for.
