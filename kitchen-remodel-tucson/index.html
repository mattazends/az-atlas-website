A kitchen built around custom cabinets

The cabinets set the look, the storage and the layout of almost every kitchen, so that's where we start. AZ Atlas Builder Group builds custom cabinets for kitchens, wet bars and built-ins, and as a family-owned, licensed general contractor (AZ ROC #344537) we also handle the remodeling work around them. You get one point of contact instead of a cabinet shop, a remodeler and a tile setter who never meet.

Why custom cabinets change a kitchen

Stock cabinets come in fixed widths, so most kitchens end up with filler strips, dead corners and uppers that stop short of the ceiling. Custom cabinets are built to your room, which means:

  • Full-height uppers or open shelving that use the whole wall
  • Drawers sized for pots, spices and utensils where you actually cook
  • Corner storage that works instead of a hard-to-reach void
  • Finishes and details you choose, from walnut to painted, slab doors to fluted panels
  • A range hood, pantry wall or appliance garage designed as part of the cabinetry

Materials and finishes

Cabinet boxes, door style and finish decide how a kitchen looks and how it holds up. Walnut and other stained woods show their grain and warm up a room. Painted doors give a clean, solid color but show chips over time in a busy kitchen. Slab doors suit modern homes, while shaker or fluted fronts add detail. Hardware, lighting under the uppers and inside glass cabinets, and how the countertop meets the backsplash all matter as much as the big choices. We settle these choices with you before anything is built, so there are no surprises when the doors go on.

Kitchen islands

An island is often the reason people remodel. A good one gives you prep space, storage and seating without crowding the walkways around it. Things to decide early:

  • Size and clearance. The island has to leave room to open the dishwasher, oven and fridge and to walk past someone cooking.
  • Sink, cooktop or neither. A sink in the island needs a drain and vent path, which is harder to run in an island than against a wall. A cooktop needs power or gas and a plan for ventilation. A plain prep and seating island is the simplest.
  • Seating. Counter overhang and stool spacing decide how many people fit comfortably.
  • Finish. Waterfall ends, a contrasting cabinet color or fluted panels make the island the centerpiece.

Layout changes

Some kitchens only need new cabinets and counters in the same footprint. Others need the layout changed: moving the sink to a window, swapping the range and fridge, adding a pantry, or opening the kitchen to the living room.

Opening a wall is where planning matters most. If the wall carries roof or ceiling loads, it needs a properly sized beam, posts and often engineering. See our load-bearing wall removal page. Our own crew handles the framing and structural work in house.

Permits for a kitchen remodel in Tucson

The City of Tucson's permit exemptions page says installing or replacing finishes like cabinets and countertops does not need a permit as long as the work doesn't affect plumbing or electrical. Moving a sink, adding circuits or removing structural walls changes that. Interior demolition that affects load-bearing walls needs a permit. Pima County and the towns of Oro Valley, Marana and Sahuarita have their own permit offices, and we check what applies to your home before work starts.

Beyond the kitchen

The same cabinet work carries into the rest of the house: a lit wet bar, a coffee station, built-in shelving, laundry cabinets or a bathroom vanity that matches. Many homeowners pair a kitchen remodel with a bathroom remodel or a wider home remodel.

How a kitchen remodel works with us

  1. Site walk. We measure, look at plumbing, power and the walls you'd like to change, and talk through how you cook and live.
  2. Design and written estimate. Cabinet layout, island, finishes and a clear scope with exclusions.
  3. Permits if needed. We prepare and submit when the scope requires it.
  4. Build. Demolition, any structural work, rough-ins, cabinets, counters and finishes.
  5. Walk-through. We check every door and drawer with you.
