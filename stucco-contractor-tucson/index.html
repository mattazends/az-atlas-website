Stucco built for the Sonoran Desert

Stucco is the skin of most Tucson homes. Done right, it handles the sun, heat and monsoon rain for decades. Done badly, or left with open cracks, it lets water in behind it, where it quietly damages the framing. We are a family-owned, licensed general contractor (AZ ROC #344537) and we do stucco repair, re-coating and full stucco systems with our own crew.

Stucco services

  • Stucco crack repair: hairline cracks, settlement cracks and cracks that keep coming back.
  • Re-stucco and stucco replacement: from lath and weather barrier to finish coat.
  • Patching and matching: blending repairs into existing texture and color.
  • Stucco for additions, casitas and new homes.
  • Repairs after structural work, window replacements or wall changes.

Reading the cracks

Hairline cracks

Thin cracks are common as stucco cures and moves with the heat. Many are cosmetic, but they still let water in over time and are worth sealing and re-coating.

Wider or stair-step cracks

Cracks you can fit a coin into, or ones that run in a stair-step pattern, can point to movement in the structure. We check what is behind them before patching the surface.

Bulges, soft spots and staining

These often mean water is already behind the stucco. That needs opening up, because the lath, weather barrier or framing may need replacing.

Why one team matters for stucco

A lot of stucco problems are really framing or water problems. Because we also do framing and roofing, we can fix the cause, replace damaged wood, rebuild the weather barrier and lath, and finish the wall without waiting on another trade.

Our stucco process

1. Inspect

We look at the cracks, test for soft spots and check parapets, windows and roof lines where water usually gets in.

2. Repair what is behind it

Damaged framing, sheathing, weather barrier or lath is replaced first.

3. Build the system back up

Scratch and brown coats are applied and allowed to cure properly, which matters in Tucson heat.

4. Match and finish

The finish coat is matched to the existing texture and color so the repair blends in.

Before monsoon season

Open cracks at parapets and around windows are the main way monsoon rain gets into walls. Our monsoon home prep checklist covers what to check before the storms arrive.
