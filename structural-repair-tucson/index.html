Structural problems we fix

Structural repair covers the parts of a house that carry weight: beams, posts, headers, load-bearing walls, roof framing and the connections between them. When one of these fails, the signs are usually cracked stucco, sagging roof lines, doors and windows that stop closing properly, or a beam you can see bending. We are a family-owned, licensed general contractor (AZ ROC #344537) and we handle structural repairs with our own crew.

  • Beam repair and replacement, including sagging garage, patio and porch beams.
  • Load-bearing wall removal and opening up layouts safely.
  • Termite and wood rot repair in walls, posts and roof framing.
  • Posts, headers and connections that have rusted, rotted or been cut.
  • Roof framing damaged by long-term leaks.

What causes structural damage in Tucson homes

Hidden water

Monsoon leaks at parapets, roof penetrations and window openings can run into framing for years. Wood that stays damp loses strength long before anything shows inside. Our roof leak repair page covers where these leaks usually start.

Termites

Subterranean termites are common across Southern Arizona and go after posts, door frames and wall plates near the slab.

Undersized or altered framing

Older additions, enclosed patios and DIY remodels sometimes leave beams that are too small or walls that were removed without a proper header. The house may hold up for years before it starts to sag.

How we approach a structural repair

Find the cause

We look at what is failing and why. Fixing a beam without stopping the leak that rotted it just means doing it again.

Engineer where needed

For significant load-bearing work we bring in a structural engineer and get the right permit, so the repair is documented properly when you sell.

Support, repair, rebuild

We shore up the structure, remove the damaged material and install new framing, beams or connections.

Finish it properly

Because we also do framing, stucco and roofing, the repair gets closed up and finished by the same team. You are not left with a fixed beam and a hole in the wall.

When to call

Do not wait on a visibly sagging beam, a crack that is growing, or wood you can push a screwdriver into. Small structural problems are usually far cheaper to fix early. If you are not sure, send photos through the estimate form and we will tell you what we see.
