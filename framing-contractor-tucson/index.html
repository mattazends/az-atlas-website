Framing is the part you never see, and the part that matters most

Every wall, floor and roof in a house sits on its framing. When it is right, nobody thinks about it. When it is wrong, it shows up as cracked stucco, doors that stick, sagging roof lines and walls that are out of square. We are a family-owned, licensed general contractor (AZ ROC #344537) and framing is core work for our crew, from a single rotted stud to the full structure of a new home.

Framing work we do

  • New construction framing for custom homes, additions and casitas.
  • Remodel framing: new walls, opened-up layouts and load-bearing wall removal with properly sized beams.
  • Repair framing: replacing termite-damaged or rotted wood, sistering joists and rebuilding damaged sections.
  • Beams and headers: beam repair and replacement, including sagging garage and patio beams.
  • Second stories: checking and reinforcing the existing structure before a second-story addition.
  • Patio covers and ramadas: framed patio covers and pergolas tied in properly to the house.

Why framing problems are common in Tucson

Water behind the stucco

Monsoon rain that gets past a failed window seal, a parapet cap or a cracked stucco joint can sit against framing for years before anyone sees it. By the time the stucco bulges or a stain appears inside, the studs and plates behind it may be soft.

Termites

Subterranean termites are a constant in Southern Arizona. They often work inside wall framing and around door frames where they are hard to spot.

Old additions and patio enclosures

Many older Tucson homes have additions or enclosed patios that were built quickly. Undersized headers, missing connectors and framing that was never meant to carry a roof show up when we open the walls.

How a framing repair works

We open the area, find out how far the damage goes, and replace or reinforce what is needed so it carries load the way it should. Where a beam or wall carries significant load, we work from an engineer's design. Then we close it up with sheathing and, on the outside, a proper stucco finish so the repair disappears. One crew handles the whole job, so there is no gap between the framer leaving and the stucco crew arriving.
